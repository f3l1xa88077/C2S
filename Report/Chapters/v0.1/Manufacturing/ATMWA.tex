It was not feasible for the team the pick and place the image sensor because of its fine pitch. Instead, I reached out to Advanced Technology \& Manufacturing (AT\&M) to complete the service for us.
\textbf{It was critical} that the image sensor packages were not opened, since their moisture level rating would prevent them from being exposed to the air for too long.
\nl
On Thursday 11th December, 2025, I prepared the handover documents, and at 3pm on Friday 12th December, I handed over the two image sensors and sensor boards.
On Thursday 18th December, 2025, I was notified that the image sensors were placed and a Purchase Order (PO) of \$70AUD+GST was required.
\nl
There was a long wait between the invoice being received, on Jan 15th, and the invoice being paid on March 14th, 2026, with confirmation of payment and collection on the 19th.
Myself and Tristan Davies were not aware of Curtin's financing system designed specifically for generating POs and paying invoices. Since I had made a PO on behalf of the team, we were not able to get the invoice paid for by Curtin because the document was not in their system.
As a result, the payment was made from the excess PAST shirt budget pool.

There were a lot of lessons learnt from this process. A summarised version can be found below:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Ensuring PCBs are delivered in appropriate packaging with correct details including technical information, PCB annotations and instructions for the installation process.
    \item Creating the PO through Curtin's internal financing system so the invoice can be paid by the university.
    \item If stated, the PCBs can be collected prior to payment once the service is completed.
\end{itemize}
