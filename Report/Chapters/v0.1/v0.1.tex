\subsection{Manufacturing}\label{sec:manufacturing-v0.1}

    The components for C2Sv0.1 were received on the following:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Element14}: This contained only the STMU5Gxxx processor. This was received on Tuesday, December 2nd 2025.
        \item \textbf{Digikey}: This included the AR0141CS CIS and Raspberry Pi lens, received Thursday, December 4th 2025.
        \item \textbf{Mouser}: The rest of the project components were in the Mouser order. This was received on Monday, December 8th 2025.
    \end{itemize}
    Components were checked off and quantities were verified on Monday, December 8th 2025. Replacement parts were ordered in later December 2025 and early January 2026.
