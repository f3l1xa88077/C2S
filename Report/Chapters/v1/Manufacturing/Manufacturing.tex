Only enough components were procured to assemble two PCBs entirely, using spare parts from v0.1 to save costs.
\nl
From an inventory mixup, TVS diodes and ferrite beads were not procured and were not available during assembly. I was able to use one ferrite bead and TVS diode from v0.1, and used a spare ferrite bead from EPIC (different footprint) to populate the second PCB.
As a second diode could not be found, the second PCB does not have TVS protection.
\nl
One of the PCBs was soldered with minimal bridges and the other had lots of bridges.
