Unlike version 0.1, vias were not a major issue since I did not have to route a BGA component, although the tight board dimensions and compact routing meant I had to use smaller sized vias in certain areas. For general purpose vias, I followed the 0.7/0.3 mm via sizing recommendation from \autoref{https://resources.altium.com/p/vias-101}{Atlium}. On smaller vias, I used a slightly smaller annular ring with 0.6/0.3 mm to avoid increased manufacturing costs of a 0.6/0.25 mm via. I ensured to keep vias spaced where possible to avoid voiding.
