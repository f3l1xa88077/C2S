Unlike for Version 0.1 in \autoref{sec:v0.1-sch-other}, only one button was used as the NRST pin was connected to the STLINK/V2 and BOOT0 could be left inactive according to \href{https://community.st.com/t5/stm32-mcus-boards-and-hardware/confused-over-correct-connections-for-boot0-nrst-and-swd/td-p/61611}{this article}. In addition, the \href{https://au.mouser.com/c/electromechanical/switches/tactile-switches/?illuminated=Non-Illuminated&mounting%20direction=Straight&mounting%20style=SMD%2FSMT&series=PTS636&switch%20function=OFF%20-%20%28ON%29&termination%20style=SMD%2FSMT&instock=y}{PTS636} was used as it was the smallest and cheapest button that appeared in the Mouser search for tactile ON-OFF SMD/SMT switches. Keeping the button size small helped making space for more larger and more space-critical components like the connectors. The 2-pin symbol also made it impossible to short VCC and GND on the pinout.
